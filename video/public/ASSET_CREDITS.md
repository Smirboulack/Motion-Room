All screenshots and 3D clips were captured from the local Motion Room application. New 3D clips were exported at 1920 × 1080 and 60 fps, then sampled at 30 fps for the film. Run, sprint and walk cycles play at their original speed. The traversal chapter uses explicitly labelled 0.65× slow motion in the YouTube film and 0.85× in the social edits. Editorial camera following only reframes the rendered image; it does not alter the performance or obstacle. The wave footage was originally exported at 30 fps. No Mixamo asset appears in the film.
