**Title**

Motion Room — Run, Sprint & Traverse in a Free 3D Animation Studio

**Description**

Meet Motion Room: a free, open-source studio for practicing 3D character animation.

Study a reference beside your own take. Pose with FK, IK and finger controls. Shape timing with joint tracks and curves, build a scene, and inspect motion with ghost poses and trajectories.

See distinct walk, run and sprint cycles, jumps, a roll, and three obstacle studies: a hand-supported vault, a hands-free hurdle and a mantle onto a raised platform. The library now contains 16 motion studies. Traversal scenes include fitted obstacles and source travel.

Bring a video or image reference, save complete editable projects, and export an animated GLB or a video. Everything runs locally. No account. No subscription.

Get Motion Room:
https://github.com/Smirboulack/Motion-Room

The trailer shows the actual application. Mannequin, locomotion and traversal performances: Epic Games, Game Animation Sample, used from a locally acquired copy under its applicable license. The supported vault uses the sample's Hurdle V2 performance; the hands-free hurdle uses its other running Hurdle performance. Motion Room supplies scene objects and demonstration edits. The wave character and source wave motion are Mesh2Motion assets, CC0 1.0. Original music; no stock tracks or third-party samples.

Gait cycles play at original speed. The traversal chapter is labelled 0.65× slow motion in this film; the social versions use 0.85×.
